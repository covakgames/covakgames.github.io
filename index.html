Shattercore

Shattercore is a lane-based, auto-shooting mobile action game. Fly your fleet across hostile planets, pick gates that grow your squad, build your own strategy with cards each run, and take down swarms of creatures and towering bosses. Four planets, one hundred levels, and a different build every run.

The game is currently in development. For feedback and questions: covakgames@gmail.com
